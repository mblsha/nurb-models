# Corrected Light Seal interfaces

The narrow high-W rim connects to Vision Pro. Its requested feature is the small inverse-T retaining lip within 5.8 mm of the leading edge. The broad low-W side connects to the face cushion and has eight shallow closed attachment pockets. Earlier interface labels were reversed; broad-side profile statistics must not be reused as narrow-side accuracy claims.

`interface-validation.json` is the canonical machine-readable acceptance result and `interface-validation.md` is its compact human-readable summary. Both are regenerated by `validate_interfaces.py` from the final STEP and record SHA-256 identities for the validator, model source, all three exports, reference GLB, alignment, retained measurements, and default parameters. The STEP generation timestamp and 3MF production UUIDs are normalized before hashing so identical exports retain the same evidence identity. The validator independently verifies the reference symmetry plane, checks post-boolean STEP symmetry, evaluates every retained narrow-interface scan side separately, and evaluates every finished cushion opening on both CAD sides.

The independent geometry-only plane fit differs from the stored reference plane by 0.0043 degrees and 0.0060 mm. Stored-plane reflected-reference p95 is 0.778 mm against a 1.0 mm threshold. This verification uses deterministic area-weighted scan samples and excludes the documented cloth and nose mask without using texture.

The narrow-interface acceptance intersects the actual STEP with four retained local planes. Each available right and left scan path is evaluated independently over the complete 0 to 5.5 mm interval and the 2.2 to 4.2 mm inverse-T lip interval. Complete-profile bidirectional p95 must be at most 0.35 mm, lip bidirectional p95 must be at most 0.30 mm, projection must be 0.20 to 0.65 mm, and the rim must widen by at least 1.0 mm from neck to shoulder. `vision-pro-narrow-lip-sections.png` remains a visualization; its older side-pooled metrics are not acceptance evidence.

The cushion-opening acceptance samples the actual final STEP trim edges on both X sides and compares them bidirectionally with the scan-derived pair measurement. Contour p95 must be at most 0.05 mm and reflected pair p95 must be at most 0.025 mm. The separately observed scan-side center disagreement must stay inside each feature's recorded 0.4 or 0.5 mm dimensional uncertainty. The retained source measurement stores pair-averaged opening length and width plus separate side-center disagreement, not raw independent per-side opening polylines. The validator therefore does not claim per-side raw contour agreement.

`face-cushion-wide-seating-measurements.json` and `face-cushion-wide-return-guides.json` retain the correctly relabeled source measurements for the broad contact surface. `face-cushion-wide-pockets.json` records paired floor centers, support centers, outward normals, long axes, opening dimensions, depths, side-center disagreement, and uncertainty. Floor and support centers differ by the measured depth along the outward normal. Dark texture without matching depression is excluded.

Run `python references/validate_reconstruction.py` after exporting the default STEP, STL, and 3MF. It checks one connected body, export manifoldness, symmetry of the actual trimmed boundary including pocket edges, measured floor positions, small nonzero fit offsets, and then regenerates and enforces the interface evidence. `validate_interfaces.py` can also be run directly when only reference agreement and interface acceptance need to be refreshed.

`alignment.json` preserves the original rigid mirror-plane fit. Original PLY, texture, textured GLB, and converter are unchanged. The original datum is corrected by one proper rigid matrix, with no scale, warp, or scan mirroring.

`face-cushion-wide-pocket-review.json` and `face-cushion-wide-pocket-thickness.json` verify actual exported floors and sampled remaining material. `face-cushion-wide-final-cad.png` shows all eight finished pockets. The 0.19 mm automatic thin-section finding is on the small narrow retaining lip, remote from these pocket floors.

Black nose-guard cloth, the light-seal interior, deep lining shoulders, magnets, ribs, cosmetic seams, and texture relief remain outside scope. Scan agreement is reconstruction evidence, not physical fit certification.
