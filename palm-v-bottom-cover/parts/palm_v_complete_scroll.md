# Palm V curved scroll rocker

## What it is

A smooth, bilaterally symmetric hourglass rocker with a longitudinal crown fitted from the original complete-scan X=0 section. The nominal visible outline runs from Y=-53.2 to -38.1 mm. Broad crown stations replace scan roughness while retaining the lower-end drop, central rise and raised upper lobe. The hidden 1.4 mm underside depth is a placeholder, not a recovered switch assembly.

## Design notes

This part belongs to the re-audited `palm_v_complete` exterior assembly. Reference coordinates use the original fixed whole-device datum.

## Don't

Do not infer switch travel, attachment dimensions, production tolerances or hidden internal fit from this exterior model.
