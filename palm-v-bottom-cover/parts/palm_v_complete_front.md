# Palm V complete front shell

## What it is

The existing independently fitted smooth front surface and its provisional sheet gauge are retained. The display and four circular application-button openings remain scan-based. The central rocker opening follows the re-audited complete-device crown footprint, and the observed power and contrast controls retain their asymmetric positions.

## Design notes

This part belongs to the re-audited `palm_v_complete` exterior assembly. Reference coordinates use the original fixed whole-device datum.

## Don't

Do not infer switch travel, attachment dimensions, production tolerances or hidden internal fit from this exterior model.
