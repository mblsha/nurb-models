# Palm V reconstruction intent

The deliverable is an editable nominal reconstruction inferred from the supplied scans, not a copy of every observed irregularity and not a recovered manufacturer drawing. The user requested the appearance and geometric discipline of the original manufactured CAD: smooth curves, clean edges, no glue traces, and no accidental bends.

The main frame and case envelope are exactly mirrored across the upright longitudinal center plane. The physical scans remain unmirrored and unchanged. The coordinate frame is fitted from corresponding outer geometry rather than mass-centroid PCA, which is biased by asymmetric internal details and unequal scan coverage.

Front and rear mating profiles are measured independently. They must not be made identical by mirroring through the thickness. The future separate front-metal scan will refine the provisional front reconstruction derived from the assembled scan.

The back cover and plastic body share nominal rear seating geometry. The intended rear interface has zero designed clearance along the supported seating runs, with no positive-volume material overlap. It is an ideal CAD contact condition, not a manufacturing tolerance prescription. The interior cavity remains open; zero clearance does not mean filling the entire space between the covers. Contact must be checked across each continuous seating run rather than inferred from a single zero minimum distance.

Intentional asymmetric features, including the rear Reset opening and clearly supported connector, switch, and internal locating details, remain asymmetric. Uncertain adhesive, scanning noise, tiny disconnected fragments, and irregular deformation are excluded from nominal CAD while remaining present in the original reference meshes.

Curved design surfaces use smooth, low-complexity profiles and continuous tangents where the manufactured part is meant to flow continuously. Rounded transitions must not overshoot into local bumps. Straight fold runs and seating ledges are constrained directly in physical coordinates. Genuine holes, shoulders, and cut edges retain their functional edge treatment.

Geometry validity, agreement with observed scans, and physical mating fit are separate claims. Scan-derived dimensions remain provisional until checked on the physical device. Intentional idealization is recorded separately from measurement residuals.

The nominal aluminium split end has level wing crests, vertically ruled outer opening walls, and long sides that stay level until a compact turn into the split return. The observed scan instead suggests a gradual ramp at that end; this correction follows the owner's stated manufacturing intent and should be reported as a local scan departure. At the opposite continuous end, the visible hem and both corner transitions descend smoothly without a small reversal, while the broad sheet keeps its transverse crown. The plastic rear seats are generated against the same nominal metal interface; contact near the inner +Y cut edge is checked separately from whole-band contact and material-overlap rays.
