# PCB

## What it is

A simple 116 by 71 mm board envelope, centered at X=2 mm, with corner cuts, two rear-clip reliefs, a lower edge relieved around the rebuilt back rail, and holes in the registered mounting pattern. Thickness is nominally 1 mm. The assembly carries the separate rectangular electronics.

## Don't

Do not interpret this as an electrical design, component footprint drawing or exact PCB edge trace. It supplies mechanical context for the shells.

## Design notes

The lower edge is limited to local rear-frame Y=-28.85 mm to clear the measured inner rail and its end bridges. The upper edge and registered screw holes are retained. This is a generic envelope adjustment, not a claim about the original circuit board outline.
