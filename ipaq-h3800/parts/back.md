# back

## What it is

A new editable rear-cover construction from the preserved iPAQ scan: crowned thin skin, rolled sides, three rounded inner rails with low interruptions, curved transverse braces, two hollow end bridges, nine asymmetric rim catches, four tapered screw seats and two bent end tabs.

## Design notes

The source coordinate scale remains provisionally millimetres. The skin uses the measured broad crown and side-roll stations with a nominal 0.4 mm normal gauge. The basic body is 116.7 by 79.2 mm before its navigation tabs and the one-sided shoulder. The positive-Y speaker corner flares outward to approximately Y=43.4 mm and curls down into the front side feature. This is an intentional mating feature identified in both scans and in the user annotation, not scan distortion. The mating outline and fixed four-hole registration remain shared with the front. The short navigation boundary is planar, as requested for the Blu Tack repair. Its normal-thickness closing face departs from that plane by less than 0.01 mm.

The new inner rail follows three observed high runs at local X [-41,-26], [-17,-3] and [5,33] mm, with a nominal Z=-6.9 mm crest. Three curved braces replace irregular scanned growth. The end platforms are hollow underneath. Added ribs and bridges are trimmed against the inner skin so they cannot protrude through the exterior. The nine catches follow the two different side patterns; the flared corner is reconstructed separately as a smooth returned lip. Each catch has a rounded top and a corresponding front receiver with 0.2 mm nominal clearance. Stepped screw recesses use 4.0 mm seats and 2.4 mm through holes, both provisional; tapered lands and post relief replace the former straight cylinders. The simplified PCB has a relieved lower edge to clear the real inner rail.

Deliberate differences from the scan are retained in the comparison: removal of support material and surface ripple; a planar repaired short edge; uniform sheet gauge; simplified smooth braces, screw details, tabs and pads; omission of uncertain label and adhesive impressions. These are nominal CAD interpretations, not an exact mesh trace or a physically proven replacement part. Original scan assets and the saved reference transform are unchanged. Assembly alignment and export checks run through references/validate_assembly.py.

## Don't

Do not restore the featureless cover with four detached-looking cylinders, short rectangular rail blocks, or internal ribs that penetrate the exterior skin. Do not fill the two low interruptions between the inner rails. Do not mirror the asymmetric shoulder or catch layout. Preserve the flared lip and its matching front underlap. Do not change the fixed reference alignment to hide local errors. Preserve the repaired flat short edge and the measured mounting centres. Do not infer print readiness from CAD validity: the nominal shell is thin and fit is provisional.
