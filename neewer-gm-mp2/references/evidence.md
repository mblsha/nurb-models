# Current Neewer GM-MP2 evidence

The motion matrix now checks requested world motion against one fixed scan-pose oracle: carriage translation, quarter-turn rotation about the independently recorded (103.5, 22.10675, 28.0) mm scan pivot, and immobility of all rail and drive components. Relative attachment checks remain additional constraints. `python references/test_commanded_motion.py` rejects frozen travel, frozen detents, reversed movement, a displaced rotation pivot, and unintended rail motion. Native feature contracts and saved viewer inspection setups can be regenerated by running `python ../prepare_inspections.py` from this project.

`neewer-arca-sections.svg` overlays the current CAD and declared PLY.GZ reference in three X-normal sections: a retained bottom dovetail station, a folded-foot bay, and the top clamp in the scan pose. The companion `neewer-current-acceptance.json` records the numerical feature checks and binds them to the exact model sources, validator source, `measurements.toml`, acceptance specification, generated component geometry, reference mesh, source STEP, identity alignment, and parameter pose.

The bottom-interface acceptance samples both contact flanks at x=5, 40, 100, and 202 mm. Their CAD requirement-to-scan p95 distances are 0.17 to 0.23 mm. Sections at the centres of both folded-foot bays independently show the flank absent, and topology probes on both sides of every transition test material immediately outside the documented ±0.25 mm uncertainty band and absence immediately inside it. The top fixed and movable jaw flanks are sampled at x=103.4 mm in the longitudinal scan pose and have 0.61 and 0.55 mm p95 respectively. All are within the card's 0.75 mm reconstruction band.

The same validator builds the four quarter-turn detents at 0, 70.35, and 140 mm travel. It compares each attached clamp component's relative Location and complete B-rep vertex set in the rotary-base frame. It also confirms the declared 0.05 mm sleeve clearance against the current 0.089 mm exact CAD gap and reopens temporary STL and 3MF exports of the exact printable sleeve. The supplied STEP is the sleeve's authoritative source and is reopened directly.

Run `PYTHONPATH=/path/to/nurb/src /path/to/nurb/.venv/bin/python references/validate_reconstruction.py` from this project to reproduce the JSON and SVG. The validator writes canonical evidence only after every child result passes; a failed run prints a failed report, exits nonzero, and leaves the last accepted artifacts untouched. `python references/validate_reconstruction.py --check-report` compares source, validator, measurements, reference, source STEP, parameters, and acceptance-specification identities without rebuilding or writing. Run `python references/test_validator_regressions.py` to prove an impossible sleeve-clearance declaration fails under both normal and optimized Python. Whole-model accuracy is not claimed: the scan, optional photo configuration and custom sleeve accessory intentionally represent different visible configurations, while fine texture and hidden mechanisms remain unresolved. Geometry validity, local scan agreement, CAD assembly fit, printability, and physical verification remain separate verdicts; physical mating has not been tested.


The 2026-09-30 detail pass adds independently fitted curved pocket ends and shallow capsule strips, rounded outer bearing housings, shaft passages, a connected rotary support and the visible 90-degree positioning lock. `references/clamp_detail_evidence.py` reproduces local contour, floor, geometry and clearance checks; `references/clamp-detail-evidence.json` records their input hashes. Hidden bores and the rotary socket are explicitly provisional. The calibrated scan pose is now `arca_detent=0, carriage_position_mm=70.35`; it preserves the observed world pivot while fitting nominal 140 mm travel between the reconstructed end blocks. Approximately 0.03 mm endpoint CAD gaps are below scan uncertainty and do not establish real stop clearance.

`references/exterior-detail-provenance.json` separates scan-supported geometry, photo appearance and nominal mechanical choices. The optional installed camera plate, six smooth pads and nominal 1/4-20 stud are absent from the scan; their photo-inferred dimensions are not manufacturer measurements. Rail scales, approximate lettering, silver heads and a dark end cap add recognizable exterior appearance. `references/exterior_detail_evidence.py` checks all 24 combinations of plate detent, carriage travel and foot state and writes runtime evidence and a reopened GLB to `build/`. The rail lead-screw pitch remains unknown and its thread is not modeled.

`neewer_feet.py` keeps the two scan-envelope feet separately named. Their hidden hinge positions and 65-degree deployment are provisional interpretations of the visible seams and product photograph. A fixed accommodation cut uses the exact continuous rotational sweep, while `references/foot-detail-verify.py` also checks all 66 integer angles for valid geometry, zero positive-volume frame overlap and preservation of the recorded Arca flanks. The model does not invent hidden hinge pins. `references/verify_endblock_receivers.py` records the separate provisional 0.05 mm radial receiver allowance around the existing independent shaft and knob axes, retains blind seats, and verifies zero shaft-to-end-block overlaps in both foot states. These checks establish internal CAD consistency, not manufactured bearing geometry or physical load capability.

Both `parts/_neewer_exterior.py` and root-level `neewer_feet.py` are included in acceptance and viewer source identity. Local fit residuals describe agreement with sampled mesh surfaces; they do not estimate scanner accuracy. The repaired scan has no colors or textures, so material appearance and approximate lettering use the official photograph as qualitative evidence.
