# Current Neewer GM-MP2 evidence

The motion matrix now checks requested world motion against one fixed scan-pose oracle: carriage translation, quarter-turn rotation about the independently recorded (103.5, 22.10675, 28.0) mm scan pivot, and immobility of all rail and drive components. Relative attachment checks remain additional constraints. `python references/test_commanded_motion.py` rejects frozen travel, frozen detents, reversed movement, a displaced rotation pivot, and unintended rail motion. Native feature contracts and saved viewer inspection setups can be regenerated by running `python ../prepare_inspections.py` from this project.

`neewer-arca-sections.svg` overlays the current CAD and declared PLY.GZ reference in three X-normal sections: a retained bottom dovetail station, a folded-foot bay, and the top clamp in the scan pose. The companion `neewer-current-acceptance.json` records the numerical feature checks and binds them to the exact model sources, validator source, `measurements.toml`, acceptance specification, generated component geometry, reference mesh, source STEP, identity alignment, and parameter pose.

The bottom-interface acceptance samples both contact flanks at x=5, 40, 100, and 202 mm. Their CAD requirement-to-scan p95 distances are 0.17 to 0.23 mm. Sections at the centres of both folded-foot bays independently show the flank absent, and topology probes on both sides of every transition test material immediately outside the documented ±0.25 mm uncertainty band and absence immediately inside it. The top fixed and movable jaw flanks are sampled at x=103.4 mm in the longitudinal scan pose and have 0.61 and 0.55 mm p95 respectively. All are within the card's 0.75 mm reconstruction band.

The same validator builds the four quarter-turn detents at 0, 70, and 140 mm travel. It compares each attached clamp component's relative Location and complete B-rep vertex set in the rotary-base frame. It also confirms the declared 0.05 mm sleeve clearance against the current 0.089 mm exact CAD gap and reopens temporary STL and 3MF exports of the exact printable sleeve. The supplied STEP is the sleeve's authoritative source and is reopened directly.

Run `PYTHONPATH=/path/to/nurb/src /path/to/nurb/.venv/bin/python references/validate_reconstruction.py` from this project to reproduce the JSON and SVG. The validator writes canonical evidence only after every child result passes; a failed run prints a failed report, exits nonzero, and leaves the last accepted artifacts untouched. `python references/validate_reconstruction.py --check-report` compares source, validator, measurements, reference, source STEP, parameters, and acceptance-specification identities without rebuilding or writing. Run `python references/test_validator_regressions.py` to prove an impossible sleeve-clearance declaration fails under both normal and optimized Python. The report intentionally excludes whole-model coverage because the editable reconstruction omits feet, threads, markings, rubber pads, fasteners, and texture. Geometry validity, local scan agreement, CAD assembly fit, printability, and physical verification remain separate verdicts; physical mating has not been tested.
